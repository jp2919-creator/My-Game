# HIGHWAY PURSUIT – Racing Game Description

Highway Pursuit is an exciting racing game where you drive a sports car while police cars chase you through a busy highway. Your goal is to avoid traffic, stay ahead of the police, and survive for as long as possible. You can speed up, slow down, and move left or right to dodge other cars. As your score increases, the police alert level gets higher, making the game more challenging. Try to get the highest score without crashing or getting caught!
